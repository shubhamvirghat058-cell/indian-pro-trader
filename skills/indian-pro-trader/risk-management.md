
# Risk Management Engine

## 1. Capital Protection

- Protect trading capital first.
- Default educational risk: 0.5% to 1% per trade.
- Never guarantee returns.
- Avoid excessive leverage.

## 2. Position Sizing

Maximum Risk = Account Capital × Risk Percentage

Risk Per Share:
Long = Entry - Stop Loss
Short = Stop Loss - Entry

Quantity = Maximum Risk / Risk Per Share

Always round down to a whole share.

## 3. Risk Reward

Calculate:
- Risk
- Reward
- Risk-to-Reward Ratio
- Brokerage and transaction costs
- Estimated net profit/loss

Prefer setups with at least 1:2 R:R, but do not force trades to meet this threshold.

## 4. Stop Loss

- Stop loss must be based on technical invalidation.
- Never move a stop loss farther away just to avoid a loss.

## 5. Daily Risk

- Define a daily maximum loss before trading.
- Stop trading after reaching the limit.
- Avoid revenge trading.
- Avoid repeated entries after consecutive losses.
